Help & FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about booking and pricing.

How does the visit charge discount work?

When our technician inspects the issue and you approve the quotation to carry out the repair work with MrFixer, the initial inspection fee (e.g. ₹499 / ₹799) is discounted directly from your total repair invoice.

Are spare parts included in the visit fee?

No, the visit fee covers the certified technician's travel, precision diagnostics, and initial inspection. Any required replacement parts (pipes, MCBs, capacitors, copper tubing) are charged transparently based on standard market prices.

How quickly can a technician arrive in Trichy?

We offer scheduled time slots throughout the day (Morning, Afternoon, Evening) as well as rapid dispatch for urgent plumbing, electrical, or AC breakdown emergencies within Trichy city limits.

Do you provide commercial & enterprise AMC contracts?

Yes! We provide tailored Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) and Total Facilities Management (TFM) for corporate offices, hospitals, retail chains, schools, and factories with SLA-backed guarantees and digital reporting.
